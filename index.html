About me.

I'm an AI engineering student who likes shipping things that are a little too ambitious for a weekend and exactly right for a portfolio. Most of my work lives at the intersection of machine learning and the web — scrapers, study tools, small interactive experiments, and the occasional late-night build that becomes a repo at 2 a.m.

I enjoy the unglamorous parts: parsing messy data, wiring things together, making something that actually runs. I'd rather have a working thing than a perfect plan. Currently levelling up in ML systems, full-stack web, and everything in between.
